Services

Fixed scope. Fixed price. Real outcomes.

Four ways to work with us, built as a ladder: start with a low-risk diagnostic and scale up only when the numbers make the case. Plus custom application design when no off-the-shelf tool fits. Every engagement is priced up front, and your diagnostic fee is credited toward whatever comes next.

Tier 0 · The front door

Planning Diagnostic

from $8,500 · 2–3 weeks

A fast, fixed-scope read on your planning health. We analyze your demand history, inventory position, and planning process, then hand you a clear picture: where cash is trapped, where service is exposed, and which fixes pay back fastest.

You keep the roadmap whether or not we work together again. Most clients use it to pick their first sprint; some use it to brief their board.

Best if: you know planning hurts but want proof and priorities before committing to a bigger project.

What's included

  • Forecast-accuracy and bias analysis on your real data
  • Inventory health check: excess, obsolete, and stockout exposure
  • Planning process and systems review
  • Quick-win list with dollar estimates
  • Prioritized transformation roadmap
  • Executive readout session

Custom Application Design
